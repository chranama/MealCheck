One-day meal plan for a healthy adult who avoids peanuts and shellfish.

Breakfast:
- 1 cup cooked oatmeal
- 1/2 cup blueberries
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt

Lunch:
- 4 oz grilled chicken breast
- 1 cup brown rice
- 1 cup steamed broccoli

Dinner:
- 4 oz baked salmon
- 1 serving sweet potato
- 1 tbsp olive oil

Prep safety notes:
- Cook chicken until fully done.
- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
