Two-day meal plan for a healthy adult.

Day 1
Breakfast:
- 1 cup cooked oatmeal
- 1/2 cup blueberries
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
Lunch:
- 4 oz grilled chicken breast
- 1 cup brown rice
- 1 cup steamed broccoli
Dinner:
- 4 oz baked salmon
- 1 serving sweet potato
- 1 tbsp olive oil

Day 2
Breakfast:
- 2 serving eggs
- 1 serving whole wheat toast
- 1 cup orange segments
Lunch:
- 4 oz tuna
- 2 cups mixed greens
- 1 tbsp vinaigrette
Dinner:
- 5 oz turkey meatballs
- 1 cup whole wheat pasta
- 1 cup tomato sauce
