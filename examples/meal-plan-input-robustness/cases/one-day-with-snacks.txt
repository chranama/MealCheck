One-day meal plan with snacks.

Day 1
Breakfast:
- 1 cup cooked oatmeal
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt

Morning snack:
- 1 serving apple
- 1 tbsp almond butter

Lunch:
- 4 oz grilled chicken breast
- 1 cup brown rice

Afternoon snack:
- 1 cup carrot sticks
- 2 tbsp hummus

Dinner:
- 4 oz baked salmon
- 1 cup sweet potato

Evening snack:
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 serving cottage cheese
